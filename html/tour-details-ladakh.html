Ladakh Mountain Expedition

Leh, Ladakh, India

4.9 (105 reviews)

7 Days / 6 Nights

Overview

Explore Ladakh's dramatic mountain landscapes, monasteries, high-altitude valleys and Pangong Lake with a paced expedition itinerary.

Tour Highlights

  • Leh local sightseeing
  • Nubra Valley excursion
  • Pangong Lake visit
  • Monastery and mountain viewpoints

Day-wise Itinerary

Day 1 — Arrival & Acclimatization

Arrive in Leh, check in and spend the day resting and acclimatizing.

Day 2 — Leh Local Sightseeing

Visit key local landmarks and monasteries at a relaxed pace.

Day 3 — Leh to Nubra Valley

Drive towards Nubra Valley with scenic mountain and pass views.

Day 4 — Nubra Exploration

Explore valley landscapes and nearby villages before an evening at leisure.

Day 5 — Nubra to Pangong

Travel towards Pangong Lake and enjoy the dramatic high-altitude scenery.

Day 6 — Pangong to Leh

Enjoy the lake in the morning and return towards Leh.

Day 7 — Departure

Breakfast, checkout and transfer for departure.
