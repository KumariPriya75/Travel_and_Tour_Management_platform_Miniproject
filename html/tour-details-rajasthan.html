Royal Rajasthan Trail

Jaipur, Rajasthan, India

4.4 (74 reviews)

6 Days / 5 Nights

Overview

Explore Jaipur's architectural landmarks, royal heritage, colourful markets and traditional culture on a comfortable heritage-focused trip.

Tour Highlights

  • Hawa Mahal visit
  • Amber Fort sightseeing
  • City Palace and old-city exploration
  • Local market and handicraft experience

Day-wise Itinerary

Day 1 — Arrival in Jaipur

Check in and enjoy an evening walk through the old city.

Day 2 — Forts & Palaces

Visit Amber Fort, Jal Mahal viewpoint and other heritage landmarks.

Day 3 — Pink City Heritage

Explore Hawa Mahal, City Palace and Jantar Mantar.

Day 4 — Culture & Markets

Discover local crafts, markets and traditional cuisine.

Day 5 — Leisure Day

Optional cultural activities and free time for shopping.

Day 6 — Departure

Breakfast, check out and transfer for departure.
