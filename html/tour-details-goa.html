Goa Beach Escape

Goa, India

4.5 (120 reviews)

3 Days / 2 Nights

Overview

Escape to the sun-kissed beaches of Goa with a balanced mix of beach time, heritage sightseeing, local food and a relaxed coastal atmosphere.

Tour Highlights

  • Beachside resort stay with breakfast
  • Old Goa heritage sightseeing
  • Sunset cruise on the Mandovi River
  • Water sports session at Baga Beach

Day-wise Itinerary

Day 1 — Arrival & Beach Walk

Arrive in Goa, check in to your resort, relax at Baga Beach and enjoy a welcome dinner.

Day 2 — Heritage & Water Sports

Visit Old Goa churches in the morning, enjoy water activities in the afternoon and explore a local market.

Day 3 — Sunset Cruise & Departure

Spend the morning at leisure, enjoy a river cruise and transfer to the airport or station for departure.
